Workflow: Upload one or more tile STLs (tiles, corners, edges) → set each stack height → download .3mf → open in BambuStudio → slice → send to printer.

How stacking works: Air gap places each tile on top of the previous one with a small gap (default 0.20mm) so a single layer of air separates them. Interface layer instead inserts a thin copy of the tile footprint between tiles, assigned to filament 2 — load a material that won't bond to your tiles (PETG between PLA tiles, or PLA between PETG tiles) and the stack pops apart cleanly with no fusing and no ironing tricks.

Tip: After opening in BambuStudio, check the plate looks correct before slicing. All tiles should be visible stacked vertically in the preview.
