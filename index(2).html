Workflow: Upload your tile STL → choose stack count → download .3mf → open in BambuStudio → slice → send to printer.

How stacking works: Each tile is placed directly on top of the previous one with a small gap (default 0.20mm). The printer deposits a single layer of air at each boundary, which is just enough for the tiles to pull apart cleanly when done.

Tip: After opening in BambuStudio, check the plate looks correct before slicing. All tiles should be visible stacked vertically in the preview.
